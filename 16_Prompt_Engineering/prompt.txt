Tokenization
    - Tokenization is the precess of breaking down text into
      smaller units called tokens. These tokens can be words, subwords, characters,
      or even sentences, depending on the level of granularity required for a specific application.

Embeddings:
    - Embeddings are vector representations of words that attempt to capture its meaning.

* Q (Query): What information is the current token looking for?
* K (Key): What information does each token contain to be matched against the query?
* V (Value): What information should be retrieved if a key matches the query?

In one line: Q asks, K matches, V provides.
